# Tax year (firm.demo)

For a US return, the tax year is the calendar year: January 1 to December 31.
Hours count toward the tax year in which they were worked, using the date of the work,
not the fiscal year of the project they are billed to.

Demo fixture for `tcxp://firm.demo/rules/tax-year`. The virtual note `!tcxp:/firm.demo/rules/tax-year`
is a different state on the same registry.
